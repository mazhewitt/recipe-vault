Lamb Rogan Josh

Prep20 min
Cook1 h 45
Serves4
Difficulty
Photo · tap to enlarge

Ingredients

800 glamb shoulder, diced
2onions, finely sliced
4 clovesgarlic, crushed
1 thumbginger, grated
2 tbspKashmiri chilli powder
1 tspground fennel
400 gchopped tomatoes
150 gnatural yoghurt
1 handfulcoriander, to finish
Serves 4 · quantities scale with the button above

Method

1
Brown the lamb in batches in a heavy pan until deeply coloured. Set aside.
2
Soften the onions in the same pan, then add garlic and ginger and cook for two minutes.
Set 2 min timer
3
Stir in the spices, then the tomatoes and yoghurt. Return the lamb, cover and simmer gently.
Set 90 min timer
4
Uncover, reduce until the sauce clings to the meat, and finish with coriander.
Cook
Rogan josh freezes well. Double it and add the extra to a shopping list?
