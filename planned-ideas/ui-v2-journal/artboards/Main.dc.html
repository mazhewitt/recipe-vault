Lamb Rogan Josh
2 h 05 totalserves 4difficulty
ingredients:
800 g lamb shoulder, diced
2 onions, finely sliced · 4 cloves garlic, crushed
1 thumb ginger · 2 tbsp Kashmiri chilli · 1 tsp fennel
400 g tomatoes · 150 g yoghurt · coriander to finish
method:
1.Brown the lamb in batches until deeply coloured.
2.Soften onions, add garlic and ginger, cook 2 min.
3.Add spices, tomatoes, yoghurt and lamb. Cover, simmer 90 min. timer running
4.Reduce until the sauce clings, finish with coriander.
